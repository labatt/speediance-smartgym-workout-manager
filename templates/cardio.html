What isn't here, and why

VO2max, cardiorespiratory fitness and resting heart rate need overnight heart-rate-variability and resting readings from a paired wearable. Speediance returns nothing for them on this account, so this page leaves them out rather than showing cards that would never fill in. Daily heart-rate and blood-oxygen trends aren't available either — Speediance's endpoints for them reject every request shape tried so far. Per-session heart rate is recorded, and shows in a session's history detail.
